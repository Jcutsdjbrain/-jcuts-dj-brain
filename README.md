# 🧠 DJ Brain v1.0.0 Beta

### Know What to Play Next.
![DJ Brain Interface](ChatGPT%20Image%20Aug%2011,%202026,%2011_03_50%20PM.png)
DJ Brain is a Windows DJ assistant designed to help DJs choose their next track faster and make smoother transitions while working alongside Serato DJ.

Instead of scrolling through hundreds of songs trying to decide what fits next, DJ Brain analyzes your music using BPM, musical key, energy, transition style, and your selected music folder or crate.

---

## 🎧 What DJ Brain Does

DJ Brain helps you:

- Find the next compatible song
- Match tracks by BPM
- Match tracks harmonically by musical key
- Compare track energy
- Keep recommendations inside your selected folder or crate
- Avoid recommending songs you already played
- Plan transitions with Transition Coach
- View cue, vocal, drop, and outro timing information
- Search for recommended songs in Serato
- Track your live set
- Save your set when finished

---

## 🧠 Smart Recommendations

DJ Brain gives you:

### ✅ GO
The strongest recommended track for your current song.

### 🔄 BACKUP
Another compatible option when you want a different direction.

Recommendations consider:

- BPM
- Musical key
- Energy
- Transition type
- BPM range
- Selected music folder / crate
- Tracks already played

---

## 🎚 Transition Coach

Transition Coach helps explain how to move from your current song into the recommended track.

Choose your transition style and DJ Brain evaluates the available tracks to help you make a smoother move.

---

## 🎵 Works With Your Music

DJ Brain uses your existing music folders.

Your files are **not moved or reorganized**.

You choose the folder or crate you want to work from, and DJ Brain can keep its recommendations inside that selection.

---

## 🎛 Serato Companion

DJ Brain is designed to work alongside:

- Serato DJ Lite
- Serato DJ Pro

DJ Brain helps choose the song.  
Serato remains your DJ performance software.

---

## 💻 System Requirements

- Windows 10 or Windows 11
- Serato DJ Lite or Serato DJ Pro recommended
- Local music files
- Internet connection required for initial license activation

---

## 🔐 Licensing

DJ Brain Beta uses license-key activation.

Each purchase includes:

- One DJ Brain license key
- Activation on **one computer**
- DJ Brain Windows installer
- Quick Start Guide
- Full User Guide
- Beta Tester Instructions

Do not share your license key.

---

## 🧪 Beta Version

**Current public beta: v1.0.0**

DJ Brain is currently in active beta testing.

Beta testers are encouraged to report:

- Bugs
- Crashes
- Buttons that do not respond
- Recommendation problems
- Serato integration problems
- Incorrect BPM/key/energy behavior
- UI or workflow issues

Screenshots and a description of what happened before the problem are especially helpful.

---

## 📦 Installation

1. Purchase DJ Brain.
2. Download `DJBrainSetup_v1_0_0_Beta.exe`.
3. Run the installer.
4. Open DJ Brain.
5. Enter the license key provided after purchase.
6. Click **Activate DJ Brain**.
7. Start mixing.

---

## ⚠️ Important

DJ Brain is a recommendation and workflow assistant.

Always use your ears, DJ experience, and crowd response when choosing what to play.
